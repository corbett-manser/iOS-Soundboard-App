# Soundboard App

**Developer:** Corbett Manser

## Overview
**iOS Soundboard** is a customizable soundboard app where every button plays a sound of your choice! Pick any audio file you have downloaded on your iPhone, assign it to a button, and play it with a single tap. Rename each button so you always know which sound you are about to play, and swap sounds whenever you would like. Happy sound-boarding!

## Features
- **Soundboard Grid:** A scrollable grid of buttons (pads), each one mapped to its own sound. Tapping a pad plays its sound instantly
- **Files Integration:** Tap an empty pad to open the iPhone's Files app and choose an .mp3 (or other audio file) from iCloud Drive, Downloads, or anywhere else on the device. The file is copied into the app so it keeps working even if the original is deleted
- **Switch Sounds:** Long-press any pad and choose "Choose Sound…" to replace its sound with a different file, or "Remove Sound" to clear it
- **Rename Buttons:** Long-press any pad and choose "Rename" to give it a custom name, so you know exactly what sound is coming
- **Rapid Tapping:** Pads are never locked while a sound is playing. Tapping a new pad immediately stops the previous sound and plays the new one
- **Add and Delete Pads:** Use the "+" button in the toolbar to add more pads, or long-press a pad and choose "Delete Pad" to remove it
- **Saved Between Launches:** Your pads, names, and sound assignments are saved on the device, so your soundboard is exactly how you left it when you reopen the app
